About Us

The Studio Story

Studio was founded in 2018 with a simple belief: great design should feel effortless. We are a collective of designers, strategists, and thinkers who share a passion for creating meaningful brand experiences.

Based in New York, we work with ambitious brands worldwide — from emerging startups to established enterprises. Our approach is rooted in strategy, driven by craft, and measured by impact.

We keep things small by design. Every project gets our full attention, and every client works directly with the people doing the work. No layers, no handoffs — just focused collaboration from start to finish.
